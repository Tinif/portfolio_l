    Quad Leg Robot Design,Simulation And Integrated With ROS

    4th Semester Project

    Designed and simulated a quadruped robot with full CAD modeling and seamless ROS integration.

    Project Gallery

    Project Overview

    The quad-leg robot project focuses on the design, simulation, and integration of a four-legged robotic system using ROS, developed during my 4th semester. It explores locomotion, stability, and coordinated movement through a structured robotic framework.

    In this project, I designed the mechanical components and learned to simulate individual parts and full system behavior, gaining hands-on experience in modeling, simulation, and ROS-based control integration.

    My Role

    • Designed the quad-leg robot using SolidWorks
    • Performed static simulations to validate part strength and stability
    • Optimized components for 3D printing
    • Conducted motion study of the full assembly
    • Simulated the robot in RViz using ROS 2 Humble

    Tools Used

    👁️ SolidWorks (CAD design & assembly)
    🔥 SolidWorks Simulation (static analysis)
    🐍 ROS 2 Humble (robot control framework)
    🚀 RViz (robot visualization)
    ⚡ URDF / Xacro (robot modeling)

    Skills Developed

    Legged robot design and kinematics understanding
    Simulation and virtual prototyping
    ROS 2 integration and robot modeling
    Motion analysis and system validation
    Design for 3D printing and structural optimization
