    Server Robot Design

    Personal Project

    Designed a complete sheet metal service robot with a fully finalized CAD model ready for manufacturing.

    Project Gallery

    Project Overview

    The server robot is designed to automate food and item delivery in restaurants, weddings, events, and shopping environments, improving service efficiency and consistency. It helps reduce human workload while ensuring smooth and reliable operations in busy service spaces.

    In this project, I developed the complete mechanical design of the robot, focusing on stability, load handling, and practical usability. The design supports real-world deployment by balancing functionality, durability, and ease of integration.

    My Role

    • Developed the complete mechanical structure with focus on stability and usability
    • Selected and planned motors, electronics, and battery system
    • Defined overall system architecture for real-world service applications
