    Solar Panel Cleaning Robot Integrated With IoT

    Final Year Project

    Engineered a solar panel cleaning robot featuring CAD design, ESP32-powered IoT control, real-time panel monitoring, and end-to-end electronic system integration.

    Project Gallery

    Project Overview

    We developed a solar panel cleaning robot designed to automate the maintenance and monitoring of solar installations, ensuring optimal efficiency without manual effort. The system can be remotely controlled through IoT, enabling real-time operation and status tracking from anywhere.

    By eliminating the need for on-site labor, especially on rooftops, the robot significantly reduces the risk of accidents and improves safety. This project focuses on combining automation and smart monitoring to provide a reliable, efficient, and safer solution for solar panel maintenance.

    My Role

    • Led the Solar Panel Cleaning Robot project as the Final Year Project Lead
    • Designed the complete system using SolidWorks and performed CAD-based simulations
    • Coordinated fabrication of components through external vendors
    • Led the development and integration of the electronic system
    • Programmed the robot for IoT-based remote operation and control
