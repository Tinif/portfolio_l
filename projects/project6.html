    Fabrication of Dual Side Shaper Machine

    5th Semester Project

    Fabricated a dual-side shaper machine as a semester project, covering full mechanical design, manufacturing, and assembly.

    Project Gallery

    Project Overview

    The Quad Leg Robot project focuses on the design and simulation of a four-legged robotic system developed during my 4th semester. It explores locomotion concepts, mechanical design, and virtual prototyping to understand how legged robots move and maintain stability.

    The robot was fully modeled and simulated using software and integrated with ROS for control and testing, without building a physical prototype. This project highlights my skills in CAD design, simulation, and robotic system integration in a virtual environment.

    My Role

    • Contributed to the design of the Dual Side Shaper Machine
    • Developed and optimized the mechanism to utilize both strokes efficiently
    • Coordinated fabrication of components through external vendors
    • Assembled the machine and ensured proper working of all parts
